# Data notice

The external VeReMi NextGen raw archives are not bundled. Download them from the official release and retain its LICENSE.txt and citation requirements: https://zenodo.org/records/19665762 . Dataset copyright is credited by the release to the Institute of Distributed Systems, Ulm University (2026).

The real-pilot example contains this prototype's derived predictions, aggregate evaluation outputs, trained models and provenance records from the two archives identified in docs/VERIFICATION.md. Those are research outputs, not an official benchmark implementation or an endorsement by the dataset authors.

The synthetic demo is generated by src/fedcontext/demo.py and is clearly labelled. It should not be represented as traffic collected from actual vehicles or as an independent validation dataset.
